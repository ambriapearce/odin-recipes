Tostones

Home

Dominican Twice-Fried Plantains. Tostones are typically served as a side dish and are a staple of many Latin American countries.

Ingredients

  • 2 green plantain
  • Vegetable oil
  • Salt

Steps

  1. Peel the plantains.
  2. Discard the ends of the plantain and cut the rest into 1 inch thick slices.
  3. Fry the plantains. Heat the oil over medium heat in a pan. Fry the plantains till light golden.
  4. Flatten the plantains using a tostonera or the bottom of a plate/glass.
  5. Fry the plantains again until they are golden yellow on both sides.
  6. Sprinkle with salt to season and serve immediately.
