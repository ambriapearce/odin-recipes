Ackee and Saltfish

Home

Ackee is Jamaica's national fruit. It is commonly eaten with saltfish in the national dish, ackee and saltfish.

Ingredients

  • Canned Ackee
  • Salted cod fish
  • 2 green scallions
  • 1 Scotch bonnet pepper
  • 1 tomato
  • Onion
  • Bell pepper
  • Oil
  • Thyme

Steps

  1. Prepare your saltfish by either soaking it in water overnight or boil and drain the salted fish 2-3 times until excess salt is removed.
  2. Once the saltfish has been boiled and drained break the saltfish into smaller pieces. Then set aside.
  3. Heat oil in a large pan over medium heat. Add onions, bell pepper, scallions, thyme and scotch bonnet. Sauté for 3-4 minutes until the onions have softened.
  4. Add the saltfish and stir for an additional minute.
  5. Add the tomato and ackee and cook for another 3-4 minutes until heated through. Season with black pepper.
