Fried Dumplings

Home

Traditional Jamaican side dish, often paired with ackee and saltfish. Fried dumplings are best served warm.

Ingredients

  • All-purpose flour
  • Salt
  • Baking powder
  • Butter
  • Room temperature water
  • Oil

Steps

  1. Add oil to a pan and set to medium heat.
  2. Mix all the dry ingredients. Whisk the flour, baking powder and salt.
  3. Add the butter into the flour mixture and mix until it forms a crumb-like texture.
  4. Add the water into the dough mixture. A little each time until the dough holds together.
  5. Knead the dough. Let the dough rest for at least 10 minutes.
  6. Form the dumplings. Roll small round portions of the dough into balls. Use your palm to slightly flatten each ball
  7. Fry the dumplings. Place a few balls in the oil at a time and cook for about 6 to 8 minutes. Flip at the halfway point until they're golden brown.
  8. Drain the oil. Transfer the cooked dumplings to a plate lined with paper towels.
